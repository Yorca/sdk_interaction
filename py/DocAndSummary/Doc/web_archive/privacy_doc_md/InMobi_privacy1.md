# LGPD

## Introduction

LGPD or [Lei Geral de Proteçao de
Dados](https://iapp.org/resources/article/brazilian-data-protection-law-lgpd-
english-translation/) is Brazil's **General Law of Personal Data Protection**
and came into effect on the 18th of September 2020. Largely inspired by
Europe's General Data Protection Act (GDPR), the LGPD establishes rules on
collecting, handling, storing, and sharing personal data managed by
organizations in Brazil.

## Complying with LGPD

Failure to comply with the LGPD can result in fines for a non-compliant
organization that trades in Brazil. InMobi is committed to supporting its
publisher and advertiser partners in their transition to comply with the LGPD.

We have updated our [Privacy Policy](https://www.inmobi.com/privacy-policy) to
indicate the type of data being processed and the purposes of the processing.
We have also implemented appropriate mechanisms to honor any rights that data
subjects under LGPD (such as the right to know, access, and deletion of
personal data) and notify the same downstream.

## LGPD Settings Under Compliance Tab

  1. Log in to the **InMobi Publisher Dashboard**.
  2. If you have traffic from Brazil, navigate to **Compliance LGPD Settings** on the **Publisher Dashboard** page.
  3. Read the LGPD compliance requirements carefully and select the appropriate option (**Yes** or **No**) to indicate whether your app falls under the age-gating requirements of the LGPD.
  4. To confirm **LGPD Compliance Settings** , select the appropriate option - **Yes, I have implemented the above,** or **No, I haven 't implemented the above**.
  5. Upgrade your connection to the latest InMobi SDK version. For mobile web, see our Mobile Web specifications. You can contact your **InMobi Account Manager** for the latest API specifications or any other query, or write us at [privacy@inmobi.com](mailto:privacy@inmobi.com).
