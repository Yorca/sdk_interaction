Mediation platforms

Meta Audience Network works with these approved mediation partners. Select one that’s right for your business according to the criteria provided. Mediation partners follow the principles of our code of conduct, which outlines best practices for enabling an open and fair ecosystem.

While we believe bidding through one of our partners is the best way for you to maximize your revenue via a competitive auction, we appreciate that publishers may just want the simplicity of only monetizing with Audience Network. To do this, simply install our latest SDK and set up your placements in Monetization Manager where they will be automatically set to ‘Accept Any Price’. Click here for more information.

If you already monetize with Audience Network alone, you don’t need to do anything - all your placements will remain on your current setting of ‘Accept Any Price’.
