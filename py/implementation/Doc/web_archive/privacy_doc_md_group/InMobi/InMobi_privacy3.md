# CCPA

## Introduction

The California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), which came into effect in January
2020, is a framework that lays down how organizations doing business in
California need to capture, store and distribute the personal information of
California residents. Similar to the GDPR in Europe, CCPA protects the data
privacy rights of California residents and puts them in control of their data.
They now have a Right to Know what's being collected and whether it's being
sold, the Right to Opt-Out from the sale of information, and a Right to Delete
it.

## CCPA settings under the "Compliance" Tab

  1. Log in to the [InMobi Publisher Dashboard](https://publisher.inmobi.com/signup) and accept InMobi's latest [Terms of Service (TOS)](https://www.inmobi.com/terms-of-service). The new InMobi TOS has CCPA-related clauses.
  2. If you have traffic from California, navigate to **Compliance** **CCPA Settings** within the Publisher Dashboard and click **Allow Data Processing**. If not applicable to your inventory, select **Restrict Data Processing**.
  3. If your app falls under the age-gating requirements in CCPA, i.e., your app is targeting children &lt; 13 years of age, turn the **Children Privacy Compliance** flag on within your app settings. If the app was already marked compliant for Children Privacy, we would automatically flag the app as compliant - &lt; 13 years for the US and &lt; 16 years for the EU. This is valid:

     * for creating new apps
     * for updating existing apps.

You can read more about InMobi's CCPA readiness
[here](https://www.inmobi.com/california-consumer-privacy-act).

Given the above, rely on a mechanism to scale and process opt-out requests.
For more information, see [Opt-Out
Mechanism](https://support.inmobi.com/monetize/privacy/us-privacy/opt-out-
mechanism).

For any queries or concerns, contact your **InMobi Account Manager** or write
us at [ccpa@inmobi.com](mailto:ccpa@inmobi.com).
